Posts are compiled as MDX, so Markdown and React components can be mixed. The component map is
`components/mdx/index.ts`; the components below are daisyUI components or thin wrappers around
them, and they can be used anywhere in a post.

## Admonitions

The `:::note`, `:::tip`, `:::important`, `:::warning`, and `:::caution` directives map to the
`Alert` component.

:::note
Highlight information that readers should not miss even when skimming.
:::

:::tip
Supplementary information that helps the reader succeed.
:::

:::important
Information that is essential to the reader's success.
:::

:::warning
Important content that carries a potential risk and needs immediate attention.
:::

:::caution
Actions that may lead to a negative outcome.
:::

A custom title overrides the localized default:

:::note[Custom title]
The label after the directive name becomes the alert title.
:::

GitHub's alert syntax produces the same component:

## Links and images

`a` is rendered by `MdxLink` and `img` by `MdxImage`. External links get `target="_blank"` and an
external icon; local images are copied and sized at build time.
