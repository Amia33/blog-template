## Protected assets

- The body is excluded from the generated collection for encrypted posts.
- Relative images may only resolve inside `content/posts/encrypted-demo/`.
- The image URL points at `/api/protected/posts?slug=encrypted-demo&path=secret.webp`.
- The route checks the session, the rate limit, and the allowlist of compiled image paths.

## Session check

```ts
const unlocked = await hasProtectedSession("post:encrypted-demo");
```
